Approach

How a project moves
from question to shipped.

  1. Step 1

    Understand the problem

    I talk to the people who have it, read the data, and write down what success looks like before anything gets drawn.

  2. Step 2

    Prototype in the real medium

    I test ideas as working code early, because a clickable prototype answers questions a static mockup can't.

  3. Step 3

    Ship, measure, refine

    Small releases, clear metrics, and follow-up work based on what users actually do.
